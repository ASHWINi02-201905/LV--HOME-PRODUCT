Buying guides

How to choose a sofa

Measure your room, and the doorway and stairs the sofa must pass through. Check seat depth and height by sitting on it if you can, and pick a fabric that suits how much the sofa will be used. Browse sofas

Choosing the right bed

Pick a size that leaves comfortable walking space on each side. Storage beds help in small rooms, and a padded headboard is more comfortable for reading in bed. Browse beds

Lighting a room in layers

Combine general light from the ceiling, focused light from a table or desk lamp, and soft accent light from a floor lamp. Warm bulbs suit living areas and bedrooms. Browse lighting
